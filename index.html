About Us

Our story

Kefify was born from a passion for traditional fermentation and natural nutrition. We craft our kefir-based products using authentic Bulgarian cultures, combining time-honoured methods with careful attention to quality.

Every bottle, tub and wheel of cheese is made with care, so you can enjoy the real benefits of kefir in a delicious way.
